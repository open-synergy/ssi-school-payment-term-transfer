=========================================
School Payment Term Transfer - Admission
=========================================

Lets the payment term transfer document target an Admission's payment terms instead of
an Enrollment's -- the same document, gates, approval, and total-conservation guarantee,
reused for the Admission side instead of duplicated.


Work Instruction
================

* `Create School Payment Term Transfer <docs/school_payment_term_transfer/index.html>`_
* `Approve School Payment Term Transfer <docs/school_payment_term_transfer/index.html>`_


Bug Tracker
===========

Bugs are tracked on `GitHub Issues
<https://github.com/open-synergy/ssi-school-payment-term-transfer/issues>`_. In case of trouble, please
check there if your issue has already been reported. If you spotted it first,
help us smash it by providing detailed and welcomed feedback.
