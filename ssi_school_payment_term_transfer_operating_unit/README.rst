=============================================
School Payment Term Transfer - Operating Unit
=============================================

Operating Unit support for SSI School Payment Term Transfer module. Adds
operating unit access control for the transactional model
school_payment_term_transfer, deriving its Operating Unit from the targeted
Enrollment.

=================

-----------------------
